# Asset ledger

The final render uses Strength Save's own app UI, captured on the current native iPhone simulator build with fictional demo training data. Final screenshot binding and hashes are recorded in source-manifest.json. The capture owner confirmed the final sources after visual QA on 2026-09-13.

Expected screenshot destinations:

- assets/pl/workout.png
- assets/pl/progress.png
- assets/en/workout.png
- assets/en/progress.png

Typography comes from the app's installed @fontsource-variable Inter and Space Grotesk packages. Both Latin and Latin Extended files are stored under assets/fonts.

GSAP 3.14.2 comes from the official npm package. Its license reference is stored under assets/vendor/GSAP-LICENSE.html.

No competing app graphics, generated interface, remote render assets, audio or stock images are used.
