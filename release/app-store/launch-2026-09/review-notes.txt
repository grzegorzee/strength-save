Strength Save is a workout log and planner for iPhone and iPad, with a companion Apple Watch app. The interface is available in English and Polish.

Use the email and password in the private sign-in fields. No SMS code or two-factor authentication is required. This dedicated account has complimentary PRO access so you can review all training features without a purchase. It contains no real person's workout or health data.

Suggested review path:
1. Sign in using email. If the consent screen appears, review the terms and privacy policy and continue. Health access is optional.
2. From Today, choose Quick workout. Add an exercise, enter weight and repetitions, and check a set. You can also choose a ready-made program from Plan.
3. Enable notifications when prompted to test the rest alert. Start a rest, lock the iPhone, and wait for the notification. Sound follows the device's notification and silent-mode settings.
4. Finish the workout. Open History to see the saved session and Progress to inspect the results.
5. PRO: normal accounts need an active subscription or trial to start workouts. Monthly and yearly subscriptions use Apple's in-app purchase system. The purchase screen includes Restore purchases. The review account's complimentary access is intentional.
6. Apple Health: open Profile and the health/integration settings, enable the optional Health features, and choose permissions. Completed strength/cardio workouts can be written to Health. Reading body mass is optional; the app asks before applying it to measurements.
7. Apple Watch: install the companion app on a paired Watch, keep the iPhone signed in, start a workout on the phone, and open Strength Save on the Watch. Check a set, confirm it reaches the iPhone, then finish the workout. Health permissions enable workout/heart-rate recording. The Watch has no separate subscription or sign-in.
8. iPad: use the same login. Planning, workout logging, history and progress use the larger screen layout. Apple Watch pairing is performed through iPhone.
